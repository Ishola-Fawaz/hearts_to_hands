# Hearts to Hands — Website Content & Structure Plan

Based on the existing Instagram presence ([@hearts_to_hands1](https://instagram.com/hearts_to_hands1)), adapted into a site structure that carries over what's working and fixes what a social feed can't do. Use this as the source of truth for page structure and content direction going forward.

---

## 1. Home

**Purpose:** First impression — establish mission, trust, and an immediate path to donate.

- Hero section: mission statement ("Serving hope, one individual at a time") + a primary "Donate" call to action
- Live progress bar for the current active campaign (target vs. raised vs. shortfall)
- Impact snapshot strip: cumulative stats since founding (e.g. families fed, total raised, people reached)
- Quick-donate tiles (₦1,000 / ₦5,000 / ₦20,000 / custom amount)
- Short teaser of recent activity with a link to the full Impact/Gallery page
- Footer: hotline numbers, email, social links, registration status

## 2. Donate

**Purpose:** Convert intent into an actual transaction — this is the page Instagram can't do well.

- List of active campaigns, each with:
  - Campaign name and short description
  - Target amount, amount raised, shortfall
  - Live progress bar
  - Dedicated "Donate to this campaign" button
- Fixed giving tiers (mirroring what's already resonating):
  - Give Hope to a Child — ₦1,000
  - Feed a Child — ₦5,000
  - Sponsor a Family — ₦20,000
  - Custom amount field
- Monthly Sadaqah Circle signup (recurring donation option)
- Integrated payment gateway (e.g. Paystack or Flutterwave): card, bank transfer, USSD
- Confirmation/receipt shown and emailed after donation

## 3. Campaigns (Archive)

**Purpose:** Show a full history rather than scattered one-off posts.

- Every past and current campaign as its own entry:
  - Name, dates active, target, final amount raised
  - Outcome summary (e.g. "25 families fed")
  - Photos from the distribution, where available
- Filter/sort by season (Ramadan, Eid, Day of Arafah, ongoing programs)

## 4. Transparency

**Purpose:** The single biggest trust upgrade a website can offer over Instagram.

- Running total: all-time amount raised vs. all-time amount spent
- Breakdown by campaign or program category
- Registration/legal status (CAC number if registered as an NGO in Nigeria — a priority if not yet done)
- Named accountable contact(s), not just a personal payment account
- Optional: link to receipts, disbursement records, or third-party audit if available

## 5. Impact / Gallery

**Purpose:** Consolidate the proof-of-work photos currently scattered across Instagram posts.

- Dated photo/video entries from distributions, sponsorships, and visitations (e.g. the Blind Centre visitation with LAUTECH Muslim students)
- Short captions with context: what was given, to whom, where
- Organized chronologically or by program

## 6. About

**Purpose:** Put a human face behind the payment account.

- Origin story — why Hearts to Hands started
- Team/founders, even briefly (names, roles, short bios)
- Mission and values (Sadaqah, community care, Islamic framing)
- Contact details: hotline, assistant line, email, physical location if applicable

## 7. Volunteer / Get Involved

**Purpose:** Capture people who want to contribute time, not just money.

- Ways to help beyond donating (distribution days, spreading the word, in-kind donations)
- Simple sign-up form
- Partnership callout (e.g. student groups like LAUTECH's Graduating Muslim Students)

---

## Content Carried Over From Instagram (Keep This Voice)

- Concrete, specific asks ("Feed 25 Families") over vague appeals
- Hadith/Quranic references, used naturally rather than decoratively
- Seasonal urgency around Ramadan, Eid, Day of Arafah, Dhul Hijjah
- Donor appreciation — consider a "recent supporters" or thank-you section (with consent)

## What the Website Adds That Instagram Can't

- Real payment gateway instead of a manually typed bank account number
- Consolidated financial transparency instead of scattered campaign posts
- Searchable/filterable campaign history
- Verifiable legal/registration status
- A recurring monthly giving mechanism (Sadaqah Circle) that's easier to manage as a subscription than as a recurring Instagram post
